Mood tags were assigned by measuring each candidate track's tempo, RMS loudness, and
spectral centroid (brightness) with librosa, then mapping those measurements to the
5 mood buckets — not by guessing from the title. This is a heuristic, like the rest of
this app's content analysis; it is not a certified or human-verified mood classification.
